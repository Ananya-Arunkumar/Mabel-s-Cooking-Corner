October 3rd, 2014 - Steak and Potatoes!

Return to home

Hello people!

Is there anything better than a warm, homemade dinner when the weather starts getting chilly? I don't think so! Today I'm sharing one of my favorite simple dinners: juicy pan-seared steak with golden, crispy potatoes. My family used to make something like this on Sunday evenings, and I still love how the whole kitchen smells when the potatoes are roasting away in the oven. It's nothing too fancy, but sometimes the simplest meals are the very best ones! I usually make a little extra because leftovers are wonderful the next day, too. Just don't forget to let your steak rest before cutting into it. I know it's tempting, but those few extra minutes really do make a difference!

Hope everyone's keeping warm this autumn!

- Mabel ♡

Prep: 15 mins

Cook: 35 mins

Serves: 2

For the Steak:

  • 2 beef steaks, about 1 inch thick (ribeye or sirloin work well)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, lightly crushed
  • 2 sprigs fresh rosemary or thyme (optional)

For the Potatoes:

  • 1 pound (450 g) baby potatoes, halved
  • 1½ tablespoons olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon paprika
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1 teaspoon dried rosemary or thyme

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Wash the potatoes and cut them in half. Toss them with the olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, rosemary, salt, and pepper.

  2. Spread the potatoes on a baking sheet with the cut sides facing down. Roast for 30–35 minutes, turning them halfway through, until they're golden and tender.

  3. While the potatoes cook, pat the steaks dry with paper towels. Season both sides generously with salt and black pepper. Let them sit at room temperature for about 15–20 minutes while you prepare the rest of the meal.

  4. Heat the olive oil in a heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Once the pan is hot, add the steaks. Sear for about 3–5 minutes per side, depending on their thickness and how well done you prefer them.

  5. Reduce the heat slightly. Add the butter, crushed garlic, and rosemary or thyme to the skillet. Once the butter melts, carefully spoon it over the steaks for another minute or so.

  6. Check that the steaks have reached a safe internal temperature of 145°F (63°C), followed by a 3-minute rest. Transfer them to a plate and let them rest before slicing.

  7. Serve the steak alongside the crispy roasted potatoes. If you have any fresh herbs left, sprinkle a little over the top. Enjoy while everything is still warm!

A Note from Mabel ♡

This is one of those dinners that always makes me feel at home. I like to eat mine by the kitchen window when it's raining outside. Terrence usually sits beside my chair and gives me his very best sad little eyes, as if he's never been fed in his entire life! Silly boy. ♡

Oh, and a little reminder: always make sure the oven is turned off when you're finished. I checked mine twice last night, and it was still warm when I came back. Probably just the old oven being silly!

- Mabel
