It's cakes and candles day!

It's my birthday today!! Another year older, and hopefully another year wiser! Although, judging by the state of my kitchen this morning, I'm not sure about that second part. I decided to make myself a batch of birthday cupcakes this year. I know it's a little silly to bake your own birthday treats, but honestly, why wait for someone else to do it? Besides, I can put as much frosting on them as I like, and nobody can tell me I've had enough sprinkles!

Terrence has been following me around all morning. I think he knows something is going on because he's been sitting beside the kitchen table with his very best puppy-dog eyes. Sorry, little man! No cupcakes for you!

I took a picture of the finished batch to remember that day. The last good birthday. There were twelve, just like the recipe states. When I looked at the picture, there were thirteen though... Guess there's a really hungry ghost on my tail ;)

It's been quite a busy morning, and I haven't even had my birthday breakfast yet. There's a little candle in the extra cupcake too, which is funny because I haven't lit any candles today.

Perhaps I'll have to make a wish anyway!

Right, enough rambling. Let's get baking!

Prep: 20 mins

Bake: 18-22 mins

Makes: 12 cupcakes

For the Cupcakes:

  • 1½ cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1½ teaspoons baking powder
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup (115 g) unsalted butter, softened
  • ¾ cup (150 g) granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • ½ cup (120 ml) milk

For the Buttercream Frosting:

  • ½ cup (115 g) unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 cups (240 g) powdered sugar
  • 1–2 tablespoons milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • A few drops of pink food coloring (optional)
  • Colorful sprinkles for decorating

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper cupcake liners.

  2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.

  3. In a large bowl, beat the softened butter and sugar together for 2–3 minutes, until light and fluffy.

  4. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Mix in the vanilla extract.

  5. Add the flour mixture in three additions, alternating with the milk. Begin and end with the flour mixture, mixing gently until just combined.

  6. Divide the batter evenly between the twelve cupcake liners, filling each about two-thirds full. Bake for 18–22 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

  7. Let the cupcakes cool in the tin for 5 minutes, then transfer them to a wire rack. Allow them to cool completely before frosting.

  8. To make the frosting, beat the softened butter until smooth. Gradually add the powdered sugar, then mix in the vanilla and one tablespoon of milk. Beat until fluffy, adding a little more milk if needed.

  9. To make a little crown on the top of the cupcakes, use a little spoon to add dollops of frosting and shape a crown on the top. It's ok if it isn't perfect!

  10. Serve and enjoy! Store leftover cupcakes in an airtight container. If the frosting contains perishable ingredients, refrigerate them and bring to room temperature before serving.
