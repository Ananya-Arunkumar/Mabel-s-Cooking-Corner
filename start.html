(That's me!)

Hey everyone! I'm Mabel Whitmore. Welcome to my little corner of the internet! ♡ If you're new here, you might be wondering what this website is all about. Well, I'm just a young woman living in beautiful Maine, trying to find her footing in the world, one recipe at a time!

I've always loved spending time in the kitchen. I first learned how to cook and bake from my grandmother, who was a wonderful cook. She started by teaching me how to make cupcakes, then pies, then soups, and eventually, just about everything else! Some of my favorite childhood memories are of standing beside her in the kitchen, watching her work and sneaking little tastes of whatever she was making. She always pretended not to notice!

These days, I live in a little house in Maine with my very best furry friend, Terrence! He's a scruffy little terrier with absolutely no manners when food is involved. Whenever I'm baking, he sits right beside the kitchen counter and watches me as if he's starving. Don't worry, though! I never give him anything he shouldn't eat. He's spoiled enough as it is!

I started Mabel's Cooking Corner because I wanted a place to keep all my favorite recipes and share them with other people. I've never been a fancy cook, and I'm certainly no expert, but I think the best food is the kind made with a little patience and a lot of love. Whether it's a simple weeknight dinner, a birthday cake, or a pie on a chilly autumn afternoon, there's something special about making food for the people you care about.

Besides cooking, I enjoy gardening, reading old cookbooks, and spending quiet afternoons with a cup of tea when the weather outside is gloomy. I have a terrible habit of buying more seeds than I have room to plant, and my windowsills are practically overflowing with little pots of herbs! Basil is my favorite, although I can never seem to keep it alive for as long as I'd like. One of these days I'll figure it out!

I'm still learning new things all the time, so this website is a little collection of everything I've tried, loved, and wanted to remember. Some recipes are old family favorites, while others are things I've come up with myself. I hope you'll find something here that you'd like to try in your own kitchen!

Thank you so much for stopping by. It really does mean a lot to me! Have a look around, try a recipe or two, and if you make something delicious, I hope you'll think of me while you're enjoying it. Make yourself at home! ♡

Lots of love,

- Mabel Whitmore
