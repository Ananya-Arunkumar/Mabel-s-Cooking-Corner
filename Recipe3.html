April 14th, 2015 - Spicy Salmon Curry!

Return to home

Hello, hello everyone!

I've got something a little different for you today! ♡ If you've been following my recipes for a while, you probably know that I have a bit of a weakness for anything warm and comforting, and this spicy salmon curry certainly fits the bill. I started experimenting with this recipe a couple of years ago when I wanted to try something new for dinner, and after a few attempts, I finally found a combination that I absolutely adore! The salmon is tender, the sauce is creamy and fragrant, and there's just enough spice to warm you up without setting your mouth on fire. Well, unless you add extra chili like I sometimes do! Hehe.

I love making this on rainy afternoons. There's something so lovely about hearing the rain against the windows while a pot of curry simmers away on the stove. Serve it over a big bowl of fluffy rice, and you've got yourself a wonderful little dinner. I hope you enjoy it as much as I do!

Sending warm wishes to you all!

- Mabel ♡

Prep: 15 mins

Cook: 25 mins

Serves: 4

For the Curry:

  • 1 pound (450 g) salmon fillets, skin removed and cut into chunks
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon freshly grated ginger
  • 2 tablespoons red curry paste
  • 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • ½ teaspoon chili flakes, or to taste
  • 1 can (14 oz / 400 ml) coconut milk
  • 1 can (14 oz / 400 g) diced tomatoes
  • ½ cup (120 ml) vegetable or fish stock
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon brown sugar
  • Juice of ½ lime
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

For serving:

  • Cooked rice, to serve
  • Fresh cilantro, roughly chopped
  • Lime wedges
  • Extra chili flakes, if desired

Instructions:

  1. Prepare all your ingredients before you start cooking. Chop the onion, mince the garlic, grate the ginger, and cut the salmon into bite-sized chunks. I always find that having everything ready makes cooking much more enjoyable!

  2. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pot or deep skillet over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook for about 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened.

  3. Add the garlic and ginger. Cook for another minute until fragrant, then stir in the red curry paste, turmeric, paprika, and chili flakes. Let the spices cook for about 30 seconds to bring out their lovely aromas.

  4. Pour in the diced tomatoes, coconut milk, and stock. Stir everything together until the curry paste is mixed evenly into the sauce. Add the soy sauce and brown sugar, then bring the mixture to a gentle simmer.

  5. Let the sauce simmer gently for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. The kitchen should smell absolutely wonderful by now! If the sauce gets too thick, add a little extra stock or water.

  6. Carefully add the salmon chunks to the curry. Stir gently so the pieces stay intact, then simmer for about 5–8 minutes, or until the salmon is opaque and reaches an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C).

  7. Remove the pot from the heat and stir in the lime juice. Taste the sauce and add salt, pepper, or a little more chili if needed. Be careful not to break up the salmon while stirring!
  8. Serve the curry over warm rice. Finish with fresh cilantro and lime wedges, and enjoy while it's nice and hot!
